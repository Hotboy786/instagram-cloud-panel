INSTAGRAM STUDIO FOR IPHONE - iOS 17 OR LATER

This is a separate native iPhone app. It does not use Render or your PC for
browsing. Accounts, login pages, login popups, and post/Reel links stay
inside the app. External Instagram app and Safari launches are blocked.
Some login providers may refuse embedded browsers; this app cannot override
their restrictions. Instagram may still require verification or re-login.

BUILD
The GitHub Actions workflow builds an UNSIGNED IPA on a hosted Mac. It
requires no Apple password or signing certificate in GitHub. Standard
public-repository hosted runners are subject to GitHub's availability and
usage policies. No paid/larger runner is configured.

INSTALL FROM WINDOWS
1. Open the repository's Actions tab and the successful Build Instagram
   Studio IPA run. Download InstagramStudio-unsigned-IPA from Artifacts.
2. Extract the downloaded ZIP to get InstagramStudio-unsigned.ipa.
3. Use the current official Sideloadly (https://sideloadly.io) on Windows,
   with its required Apple software, to sign and install the IPA yourself.
   Enter Apple credentials only in your signing tool, never GitHub or chat.
4. Follow iPhone's normal developer-mode and developer-trust requirements.
5. Open Instagram Studio. Tap Accounts, add a name, and sign in directly.
6. Add a second account and test switching. Restart the app and confirm
   each account keeps its own session before adding all your accounts.

Free Apple signing normally expires after seven days, requiring renewal.
Daily account browsing runs on the iPhone; free signing still needs periodic
refresh. Use the same Apple account and bundle identifier for updates.
Do not uninstall the app if you want to retain its data. Back up the device.

PROFILES
Every account has a stable UUID and a separate persistent WebKit data store.
Only one main browser is retained at a time to limit memory usage. Metadata
is stored locally, with no password collection, server sync or cookie export.
Your Windows/Render sessions are not imported. Sign in once on the phone.
Your brother can install the app but signs in separately on his own device.
The app imposes no numerical profile cap; device storage and WebKit limits apply.

NOT A GUARANTEE
Compilation and metadata tests do not validate live Instagram login, video,
or upload compatibility. Test those on the real iPhone. No automated likes,
automatic account rotation, CAPTCHA bypass, or permanent-login guarantee.
